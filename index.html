Before you ask

Questions

Do I need to be any good?

No. Competitive nights get split into two lobbies by rank so games stay close, and casual nights don't sort at all. Roughly half the club has never played ranked.

What if I can only make one night a week?

That's most people. The schedule is fixed precisely so you can pick the one night that works and ignore the rest. There's no attendance minimum.

Do I have to talk on voice?

Only on scrim nights, where callouts matter. Everything else runs fine with text — plenty of members sit muted in the voice channel and type.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Tournament entry fees, when we run one, get split between whoever's playing and are announced two weeks ahead.
